PhD defense

08/04/2026

I am excited to share that I have successfully defended my PhD thesis, "New insights into reactions induced by cluster-structured nuclei: the special cases of 12,13C" at the University of Seville.

The committee awarded me the highest grade of Summa Cum Laude. In addition, my thesis was recognized with the International Mention and the Industrial Mention. I am deeply grateful to my advisors, the committee members, and everyone who supported me throughout these years. On to the next challenge!
